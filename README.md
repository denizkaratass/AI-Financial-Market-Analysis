# 📈 **AI Financial Market Analysis**

This project performs a professional **Financial Market Analysis** focusing on leading Artificial Intelligence (AI) companies. It explores the relationship between R&D expenditures, AI-driven revenue growth, and the stock market's reaction to major technological milestones.

## 🎯 **Project Objectives**
The analysis is structured to provide deep financial insights into tech giants:
* **Financial Comparison:** Analyzing the conversion rate of R&D investments into actual AI revenue.
* **Event Impact Studies:** Evaluating how specific milestones like the **GPT-4 release** or **TensorFlow open-sourcing** affected stock prices.
* **Growth Trends:** Tracking annual AI revenue growth rates (`AI_Revenue_Growth_%`) across different organizations.
* **Statistical Correlation:** Using Heatmaps to examine the link between spending, revenue, and market impact.

## 🚀 **Key Technical Features**
- **Data Engineering:** Converting raw date strings into Time-Series format and creating yearly categorical features.
- **Advanced Visualizations:**
    - **Seaborn Heatmaps:** To visualize complex correlation matrices.
    - **Pairplots:** For multivariate analysis of all financial metrics.
    - **Scatter & Line Plots:** To track market trends and growth anomalies.
- **Granular Data Filtering:** Segmenting the dataset by company and specific "Event" triggers for detailed analysis.

## 🛠️ **Tech Stack**
- **Language:** Python
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook
- **Main Libraries:** `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Seaborn`, `Matplotlib`.
